ENER – Electricity and Energy Management

The Electricity and Energy Management (ÉMÉ) specialization within the Bachelor of Technology in Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing (GEII) trains students to understand, install, inspect, and maintain electrical systems. It focuses in particular on the generation, conversion, distribution, and use of electrical energy.

This specialization combines scientific knowledge, such as mathematics and physics, with technical courses and hands-on practical work. It prepares students to take part in the design, testing, implementation, and maintenance of electrical installations and systems.

What will you learn in this specialization?

Electrical Installations

Students learn to understand how electrical installations work, identify their components, and check that they operate correctly. They study equipment, electrical distribution, and safety regulations in particular.

Electrical Energy Conversion

This area examines how electrical energy is converted to meet the needs of a system. It helps students understand the role of power electronic converters, electric motors, and related equipment.

Electrical Machines

Students study how motors and other electrical machines operate. They learn to analyze their characteristics and understand how they are used in industrial equipment.

Energy Management and Efficiency

This area focuses on the efficient use of electrical energy, energy storage, and the study of energy systems. It helps students understand how to improve the operation of an installation and reduce energy losses.

Measurements, Testing, and Maintenance

Students use measuring instruments to monitor electrical quantities, test systems, and troubleshoot faults. They learn to interpret results and contribute to keeping installations in good working order.
