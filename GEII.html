Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing

What Is GEII?

GEII is a French acronym for "Génie Électrique et Informatique Industrielle", which translates as Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing. It is a three-year undergraduate programme combining electrical engineering, electronics, automation and industrial computing. The programme equips students with the skills needed to design, install, operate, maintain and improve electrical and electronic systems used in industry, energy, transport and maritime technologies.

The programme combines academic knowledge with hands-on experience, helping students develop the technical and professional skills required by modern industries.

What Do Students Study?

ENER - Energy and Electrical Engineering

This field focuses on electrical power systems, energy conversion and distribution. Students study electrical installations, electric motors, power electronics, energy efficiency and electrical safety. These skills are useful in industrial facilities, power networks, renewable energy and the maritime sector.

ELEN - Electronics

Electronics focuses on the design, analysis and testing of electronic circuits and systems. Students learn about analogue and digital electronics, sensors, signal processing, instrumentation and embedded electronic systems. These technologies are used in communication systems, industrial equipment and onboard electronics.

AUTO - Automation and Industrial Computing

This field combines automation, programming and industrial control systems. Students learn how to program industrial controllers, monitor machines, process data and control automated equipment. Applications include manufacturing lines, robotics, industrial networks and automated systems onboard ships.

How Are Students Taught?

Tutorials (TD)

Tutorials allow students to work on exercises, calculations and technical problems related to electrical engineering, electronics, automation and industrial computing. They help students strengthen their understanding and develop problem-solving skills.

Practical Sessions (TP)

Practical sessions take place in laboratories and workshops. Students use measuring instruments, electronic components, electrical equipment and programmable controllers to build, test and analyse electrical and electronic systems.

Projects and Practical Assignments

Projects are an important part of the programme. Students work individually or in teams to design, build, program and test technical solutions. These projects bring together several subjects and help students develop independence, teamwork, project management and problem-solving skills.

Skills and Competencies

Throughout the programme, students progressively develop the ability to:

Design

Analyse systems, develop technical solutions, create prototypes and prepare design documentation.

  • AC11.01 - Produce a functional analysis of a simple system.
  • AC11.02 - Build a prototype for hardware and/or software technical solutions.
  • AC11.03 - Write a manufacturing file based on a design file.
  • AC21.01 - Propose technical solutions based on functional analysis.
  • AC21.02 - Assess and reduce risks associated with selected technical solutions.
  • AC31.01 - Contribute to writing technical specifications.
  • AC31.02 - Demonstrate the relevance of technological choices.
  • AC31.03 - Produce a design file.
Test and Verify

Perform tests and measurements, identify malfunctions and verify system compliance.

  • AC12.01 - Apply a testing procedure.
  • AC12.02 - Identify a malfunction.
  • AC12.03 - Describe a malfunction.
  • AC22.01 - Identify the tests and measurements required to validate system operation.
  • AC22.02 - Certify the operation of new industrial equipment.
  • AC32.01 - Evaluate the root cause of a malfunction.
  • AC32.02 - Propose a corrective solution to a malfunction.
  • AC32.03 - Produce a testing procedure to validate system compliance.
Maintain (ELEN)

Inspect systems, diagnose faults, perform maintenance and recommend corrective solutions.

  • AC23.01 - Perform system servicing and inspection according to a procedure.
  • AC23.02 - Perform corrective, preventive or improvement maintenance.
  • AC23.03 - Diagnose a system malfunction.
  • AC23.04 - Identify the root cause of a malfunction.
  • AC33.01 - Propose a maintenance solution.
  • AC33.02 - Assess system downtime and maintenance costs.
  • AC33.03 - Produce a maintenance procedure.
  • AC33.04 - Provide technical support to stakeholders nationally and internationally.
Integrate (AUTO)

Install and commission systems according to procedures and ensure their compliance.

  • AC24.01AII - Follow the procedure for installing a system.
  • AC24.02AII - Commission a system according to the prescribed procedure.
  • AC34.01AII - Plan the installation and commissioning of new equipment.
  • AC34.02AII - Develop a system installation and commissioning procedure.
  • AC34.03AII - Produce the system compliance file and manage version control.
Install (ENER)

Apply installation procedures, plan commissioning and prepare compliance documentation.

  • AC24.01EME - Follow the procedure for installing a system.
  • AC24.02EME - Commission a system according to the prescribed procedure.
  • AC34.01EME - Plan the installation and commissioning of new equipment.
  • AC34.02EME - Develop a system installation and commissioning procedure.
  • AC34.03EME - Produce the system compliance file and manage version control.

A Programme Connected to Industry

The GEII programme maintains links with companies and organisations working in electrical engineering, electronics, energy, industrial automation and maritime technologies. Industry connections help students discover professional careers and understand the needs of employers through projects, work placements, company visits and professional events.

Industry Partners and Professional Organisations

Companies and organisations connected with the department's professional network include:

  • Naval Group - naval defence and shipbuilding
  • Thales - electronics and advanced technology
  • Groupe SNEF - electrical engineering and industrial services
  • Enedis - electricity distribution
  • Ifremer - marine science and ocean research
  • VINCI - construction, energy and infrastructure
  • SPIE - electrical engineering and technical services
  • Equans - energy and technical services
  • Actemium - industrial engineering and automation
  • Eiffage - construction and energy systems

These organisations operate in fields related to the skills taught in GEII. Their involvement may include recruitment events, work placements, apprenticeships, projects or other professional exchanges. The type of relationship varies from one organisation to another.

Recognised by CINav

CINav, the French National Campus for Maritime Industries, brings together companies, training providers and other organisations in the maritime sector. Its "by CINav" label recognises training programmes that include knowledge and skills relevant to maritime industries.

This maritime focus is particularly relevant in Brest, where naval engineering, shipbuilding, marine electronics and offshore technologies offer opportunities for graduates.
