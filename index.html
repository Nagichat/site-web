Understanding the BUT Degree

What is a BUT?

A French University Degree

The BUT, which stands for "Bachelor Universitaire de Technologie", is a French university degree awarded by Institutes of Technology (IUTs). It combines theoretical knowledge with practical learning and prepares students for careers in technical and professional fields.

How Long Does It Take?

The BUT takes three years to complete after high school, corresponding to six semesters and 180 ECTS credits. It is equivalent to a bachelor's degree and provides students with the opportunity to enter the job market or continue their studies.

A Competency-Based Approach

Learning Through Skills and Practice

The BUT uses a competency-based approach. Instead of focusing only on theoretical knowledge, students learn how to apply what they know to real-life situations. They progressively develop professional skills through practical work, laboratory sessions, projects and work placements.

Projects and Professional Situations

Throughout the programme, students work on projects that reflect real professional challenges. These activities help them develop technical skills, problem-solving abilities, teamwork and autonomy. Their progress is assessed through different types of coursework and practical assignments.

The GEII Programme

Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing

GEII stands for "Génie Électrique et Informatique Industrielle". This BUT programme focuses on electrical engineering, electronics, automation, industrial computing and embedded systems.

What Can You Do After a BUT GEII?

After completing a BUT GEII, graduates can enter professional life or continue their studies. Possible career paths include electronics, electrical engineering, industrial automation and embedded systems. Further studies may include engineering schools or specialised programmes.
