What Can You Do After a GEII Degree?

The Bachelor Universitaire de Technologie in Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing (BUT GEII) offers several opportunities after graduation. Students can continue their studies at an engineering school or university, or enter the workforce directly.

Career and study opportunities depend on the chosen pathway, acquired skills, academic results and each student's career goals.

1. Further Studies

Engineering Schools

After completing the GEII degree, students can apply to engineering schools to earn an engineering degree, usually after three additional years of study. Admission depends on each school's requirements and selection process.

Possible specialisations include:

  • Electrical engineering and power systems;
  • Electronics and embedded systems;
  • Automation and robotics;
  • Industrial computing;
  • Energy and renewable energy;
  • Telecommunications and digital systems.

Applications may be assessed through academic records, interviews or entrance examinations. Requirements vary between institutions.

Examples of Engineering Schools

The following institutions are examples to explore when looking for an engineering specialisation related to GEII:

  • ENIB – electronics, computing and industrial systems.
  • Polytech Nantes – engineering programmes in various scientific and technical fields.
  • INSA Rennes – engineering education in science and technology.

This list is provided for guidance only. Not every specialisation is necessarily accessible directly after the GEII degree. Check each school's admission requirements and available programmes.

2. Career Opportunities After GEII

Electrical Engineering and Energy

Professionals in this field work with electrical installations, power distribution, industrial equipment and energy efficiency.

  • Electrical engineering technician;
  • Electrical maintenance technician;
  • Renewable energy technician;
  • Electrical design technician.
Electronics and Embedded Systems

This field involves testing, developing, troubleshooting and maintaining electronic circuits, boards and embedded systems used in technical equipment.

  • Electronics technician;
  • Embedded systems technician;
  • Testing and measurement technician;
  • Instrumentation technician.
Automation and Robotics

Professionals in this field programme, install and maintain automated systems and industrial robots used in manufacturing and production.

  • Automation technician;
  • Industrial robotics technician;
  • Industrial computing technician;
  • Commissioning technician.
