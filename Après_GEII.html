What Can You Do After a GEII Degree?

The Bachelor Universitaire de Technologie in Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing (BUT GEII) offers several opportunities after graduation. Students can continue their studies at an engineering school or university, or enter the workforce directly.

Career and study opportunities depend on the chosen pathway, acquired skills, academic results and each student's career goals.

1. Further Studies

Engineering Schools

After completing the GEII degree, students can apply to engineering schools to earn an engineering degree, usually after three additional years of study. Admission depends on each school's requirements and selection process.

Possible specialisations include:

  • Electrical engineering and power systems;
  • Electronics and embedded systems;
  • Automation and robotics;
  • Industrial computing;
  • Energy and renewable energy;
  • Telecommunications and digital systems.

Applications may be assessed through academic records, interviews or entrance examinations. Requirements vary between institutions.

Examples of Engineering Schools

The following institutions are examples to explore when looking for an engineering specialisation related to GEII:

  • ENSEA – electronics, embedded systems and computing.
  • ENIB – electronics, computing and industrial systems.
  • Polytech Nantes – engineering programmes in various scientific and technical fields.
  • INSA Rennes – engineering education in science and technology.
  • UTC – engineering programmes related to electrical engineering, computing and systems.

This list is provided for guidance only. Not every specialisation is necessarily accessible directly after the GEII degree. Check each school's admission requirements and available programmes.

University Master's Degrees and Other Programmes

Students may also apply for further university studies, depending on their background and the programme's admission requirements.

  • Master's degree in electronics;
  • Master's degree in energy and electrical engineering;
  • Master's degree in automation and industrial systems;
  • Specialised programmes in industrial computing.

The BUT GEII is a three-year degree corresponding to bac+3 level in the French education system. Students should check the entry requirements of each programme before applying.

2. Career Opportunities After GEII

Electrical Engineering and Energy

Professionals in this field work with electrical installations, power distribution, industrial equipment and energy efficiency.

  • Electrical engineering technician;
  • Electrical maintenance technician;
  • Renewable energy technician;
  • Electrical design technician.
Electronics and Embedded Systems

This field involves testing, developing, troubleshooting and maintaining electronic circuits, boards and embedded systems used in technical equipment.

  • Electronics technician;
  • Embedded systems technician;
  • Testing and measurement technician;
  • Instrumentation technician.
Automation and Robotics

Professionals in this field programme, install and maintain automated systems and industrial robots used in manufacturing and production.

  • Automation technician;
  • Industrial robotics technician;
  • Industrial computing technician;
  • Commissioning technician.
Industrial Computing and Networks

These jobs combine computing, electronic equipment and communication between machines. They help ensure the proper operation and supervision of industrial systems.

  • Industrial computing technician;
  • Industrial networks technician;
  • Industrial systems supervision technician;
  • Communication systems technician.
Maintenance, Testing and Production

These activities aim to ensure the reliability, safety and proper operation of technical equipment.

  • Industrial maintenance technician;
  • Quality control technician;
  • Production technician;
  • Testing and validation technician.
Becoming an Engineer After Further Studies

After completing an engineering degree or another suitable qualification, graduates can pursue roles involving more responsibility in design, development, project management and technical supervision.

  • Electrical engineer;
  • Electronics engineer;
  • Automation engineer;
  • Embedded systems engineer;
  • Energy engineer;
  • Industrial robotics engineer.

3. Industries and Sectors

The skills acquired during the GEII degree can be used in a wide range of industries, including:

  • Manufacturing and industrial production;
  • Energy and electrical power networks;
  • Aerospace and aviation;
  • Automotive and transportation;
  • Telecommunications;
  • Robotics and industrial automation;
  • Buildings and electrical installations;
  • Research, testing and development.

4. Choosing Your Next Step

I Want to Attend an Engineering School

Research parallel admissions, available specialisations, academic requirements and work-study opportunities. Compare several schools before applying.

I Want to Start Working After Graduation

Look for job opportunities related to your chosen pathway, internships and technical skills. Available positions vary according to the employer and the experience required.

I Want to Study Through a Work-Study Programme

Some engineering schools offer apprenticeship programmes. These allow students to combine academic studies with professional experience in a company. Availability and admission requirements vary between schools.
